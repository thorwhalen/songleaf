songleaf.snippets¶

Score snippets on the sheet (songleaf#12): which stretch of a song, and a small staff for it.

A snippet is a few bars of notation drawn above one line of the lyrics, for a sense of the melody or the rhythm. Three parts, each replaceable:

  • Which stretch: choose_segment() picks a line by rule (the first line of the chorus, else the most repeated line, else the first line), and mark_segment() records the choice, with the notation, as the song’s

    score annotation (songleaf.model.score_link()). A recorded annotation wins over the rule, so it is how a choice is overridden.

  • What to draw: a snippet provider, song -> ScoreSnippet | None, is injected into songleaf.render.render_sheet() (snippets=) and never imported by it. abc_snippet() draws the short ABC notation recorded on the annotation with reportlab lines only: one voice, treble staff, notes, rests, accidentals, bar lines and chord names (no clef is drawn). With the score extra, songleaf.engrave.engraved_snippet() engraves ABC, MusicXML or MEI

    properly; default_provider() picks it when installed.

  • Where it goes: the score layout option makes the renderer place the snippet as a row above its line; the font fit counts its height, so the lyrics shrink to make room.

ABC subset read by parse_abc(): notes A-G a-g with , / ' octave marks, ^ _ = accidentals, lengths 2 /2 3/2 / (the unit is an eighth note), rests z, bar lines | || |] |: :|, and "G" chord names over the next note. A length that needs a tie (5) is refused. Header lines (K:G), decorations (!fermata!) and anything else (ties, slurs, spaces) are skipped.

>>> [(n.pitch, n.length) for n in parse_abc("C2 D/2 z |").notes]
[(28, 0.25), (29, 0.0625), (None, 0.125)]
